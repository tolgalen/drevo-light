Drevo Tyrfing V2 — Keyboard manual

Summary of the functions of the keyboard itself (no software needed), based on the official Drevo manual. To control the lighting from the computer, see the Drevo Light manual.

The FN key gives many keys a second function. While you hold FN, the keys that support an “FN+” combination light up.

3. Colors, brightness and speed

CombinationAction
FN+PrtScThe light of the trigger areas changes between 9 single colors. In Spectrum mode it switches the backlight of the whole keyboard to a single color.
FN+ScrLkThe background light color changes between 9 single colors.
FN+PauseThe light of the trigger areas shows colorful effects.
FN+← / →Adjusts the light response speed (except in light-on mode).
FN+↑ / ↓Adjusts the brightness.

4. Custom backlight

  1. Press FN+Backspace: the - and = keys (two slots) start blinking rapidly.
  2. Press one of them to choose it, then press the keys you want to light to design your own backlight.
  3. Press FN+Backspace again to finish and save the setting.

5. Macros

  1. Press FN+F12: the five keys Y U I O P (five slots) start blinking rapidly.
  2. Press one of them and start recording your macro.
  3. Press FN+F12 again to finish and save it.
  4. To use your macros, press FN+Y to activate them.
